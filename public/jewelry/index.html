Why server-side matters

Most shoppers browse on iPhones, where browser-only tracking loses a large share of the signal. Shopify's native Meta integration sends both — browser pixel and server-side Conversions API — so audiences and reports stay accurate.

What you can do with it

  • Retarget viewers who didn't buy
  • Build lookalikes from real purchasers
  • See cost per purchase in Ads Manager, per product
  • Run separate campaigns per brand
